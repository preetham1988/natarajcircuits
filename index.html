FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No. We build prototypes from a single board, and scale to thousands for production runs.

Which file formats do you accept?

Gerber RS-274X with Excellon drill files is preferred. We also accept ODB++ and IPC-2581. For assembly, please include a BOM and pick-and-place (CPL) file.

Do you ship outside Karnataka?

Yes — we ship to every state and union territory in India via express courier, with tracking shared as soon as your order is dispatched.

Will I receive a GST invoice?

Yes. Every order comes with a GST invoice so registered businesses can claim input tax credit.

How do you keep my design files confidential?

Your files are used only to manufacture your order and are never shared with third parties. We are happy to sign an NDA on request.

What payment options are available?

UPI, NEFT/RTGS bank transfer, and cards. Purchase orders are accepted for registered businesses and institutions.
